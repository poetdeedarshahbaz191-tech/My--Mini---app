🌹 About Poet Deedar Shahbaz 🌹

Welcome to the official website of Poet Deedar Shahbaz. This website is dedicated to original Sindhi poetry, especially ghazals. Every poem is written with sincerity and reflects emotions, love, hope, culture, and the beauty of the Sindhi language.

Our mission is to preserve and promote Sindhi literature by sharing meaningful poetry with readers around the world. This website currently contains more than 53 original ghazals, and new poetry will continue to be published regularly.

Thank you for visiting, reading, liking, sharing, and supporting Sindhi poetry.
